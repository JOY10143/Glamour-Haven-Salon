Do I need to book an appointment before visiting?

While walk-in clients are always welcome, we recommend booking an appointment in advance to guarantee your preferred date and time.

What services do you offer at Glamour Haven Salon?

We offer professional hair styling, braiding, colouring, facials, spa treatments, massages, manicures, pedicures, acrylic nails, makeup services, and bridal beauty packages.

Do you provide bridal beauty packages?

Yes! We offer complete bridal packages that include hairstyling, professional makeup, luxury nail services, and beauty consultations to make your special day unforgettable.

Which payment methods do you accept?

We accept Cash, M-Pesa, and other convenient payment methods. Feel free to contact us if you have any payment-related questions.

Where is Glamour Haven Salon located?

Glamour Haven Salon- Hair, Beauty & Spa is conveniently located in Kakamega. Visit our Contact section for our location, business hours, and contact details.

Still Have Questions?

Our friendly team is always ready to assist you. Whether you need help choosing a service, booking an appointment, or learning more about our beauty packages, we're just a message away.
